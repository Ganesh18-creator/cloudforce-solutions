### Quick Deploy (5 minutes):

1. **Go to**: [https://vercel.com](https://vercel.com)

2. **Sign in with GitHub**

3. **Import Project**:
   - Click "Add New..." → "Project"
   - Select "Import Git Repository"
   - Choose **"cloudforce-solutions"**
   - Click "Import"

4. **Deploy**:
   - Vercel auto-detects Next.js
   - Click "Deploy"
   - Wait 2-3 minutes
   - ✅ **Your site is LIVE!**
